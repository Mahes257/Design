This guide describes the implemented frontend only. Records, approvals, releases, transfers and handovers are browser-local simulation data; no backend, purchase order, production order or legally binding approval is created.

1. Purpose and how to use this guide

This document explains what each workspace area is for, which tabs and registers it contains, which actions are available, and where a record moves next. It is written for design engineers, design leads, reviewers, transfer coordinators and administrators who need a single map of the application.

Use the module sections as a reference while working. Use the separate End-to-End Workflow Guide for the sequence of work, the Functional Testing Report for verified results, the Convert & Action Menu Audit for menu-level coverage, and the Issues & Fixes Report for changes made during this review.

1.1 Navigation model

The sidebar groups work by lifecycle: Customer & Sales, Project & Engineering, Engineering Approval & Release, Manufacturing Preparation, Manufacturing Execution Tracking, Delivery & Service Tracking, and Administration. A module’s submodules are horizontal, route-backed tabs inside the module page, not nested sidebar links. Each tab has a direct URL and survives refresh.

Common page behaviour includes breadcrumbs, module and tab headers, KPI cards, search and filters, sortable tables, pagination, status pills, row-level three-dot menus, modal forms, validation messages and toast feedback. Wide registers scroll inside their table container rather than stretching the page.

Data boundary. The application stores working state in browser localStorage under md-*-state-v1 keys. Refreshing the browser preserves records on the same machine and browser profile. Clearing site data, changing browsers or using a private window resets the workspace to seeded data.

2. Dashboard

The dashboard is a read-oriented command centre. It presents active projects, open design tasks, drawings in review, pending approvals, open BOM value, project progress, task status, engineering workload and monthly planned-versus-actual progress. It also shows drawing repository, transfer and released-package summaries, a pending approval queue and a recent activity feed.

Dashboard figures are seeded management indicators, not a live aggregation of every local register. After creating the E2E records, the dashboard still displayed the original headline KPI values; this is a documented analytics limitation rather than a workflow failure.

5. Design Planning

Design Planning creates the plan and task layer for a project. The Design Plans tab registers plan ID, project, scope, owner, dates, status and progress. The Tasks & Progress tab registers task ID, project, design item, assignee, discipline, priority, dates, estimated hours, progress and workflow state.

Available actions include create plan, edit plan, delete plan, create task, update task status and progress, view plan and view task context. Plans and tasks are project-linked; the E2E plan DPL-2026-009 and task TSK-2105 were created against PRJ-2026-042.

6. Conceptual Design

The Concept Register captures alternative concepts with concept ID, project, concept name, description, design intent, advantages, limitations, risk, estimated cost, status and revision. The Concept Details tab provides a deeper workspace for comparison criteria, layout files, studies, proposals and selection.

Concepts can be edited, duplicated through the normal form flow, compared and selected. Selection establishes the preferred concept for detailed design. For the E2E project, CON-2026-011 and CON-2026-012 were compared and CON-2026-011 was selected.

9. Manufacturing Design

Manufacturing Design translates the design into production definition. The Manufacturing Definition tab captures process route, material, special characteristics, inspection needs, tooling, notes and status. Inspection & Quality Requirements records inspection points, methods, acceptance criteria, gauges and quality references. Process Planning & Instructions records operation sequence, work instructions, setup, cycle references and attachments.

Definition records can be viewed, their status history inspected, and their manufacturing context reviewed. MFD-2026-016 was created for the E2E part and submitted for review.

13. Engineering Change & Release

The Change Register lists engineering change requests with project, affected drawing/BOM/part, reason, priority, owner, status and revision. ECR / ECO provides impact assessment, disposition, approval and revision update. Engineering Release shows release readiness and blockers for controlled handover.

ECR-2026-008 recorded an E2E frame guard clearance correction, completed impact assessment and ECO approval, and applied a revision update. Release readiness is deliberately blocked when the referenced BOM or drawing is not approved or released.

15. Manufacturing Readiness & Handover

The Manufacturing Readiness tab records package reference, project, revision, owner, readiness checks, required documents, engineering issues, blockers and status. Purchase Handover Tracking and Production Handover Tracking record destination acknowledgement and outstanding actions without creating ERP transactions.

Readiness is a gate: released BOM, approved drawing set, package index, material specifications, inspection requirements and handover cover sheet must be recorded. The review created readiness record MR-2026-005 for the seeded approved package to exercise the workflow.

16. Manufacturing Execution Tracking

This is a read-only tracking workspace. Material & Inventory Status shows material readiness and shortages; Production Progress shows operation and completion state; Inspection & Testing Status shows inspection, test and NCR references; Manufacturing Support & As-Built shows production queries and as-built references.

Project and stage filters are available. The tracking dataset is seeded mock data and is not automatically populated by new design projects or transfers; this is an intentional read-only demonstration boundary.

17. Manufacturing Support & As-Built

The support workspace registers production engineering cases with case number, project, type, priority, owner, status, linked drawing/BOM/part/assembly, description and comments. Actions: view, edit, add comment and close case. Case project lookup now uses the live project register, so newly created projects are selectable.

MSC-Q-2026-004 was created for PRJ-2026-042, linked to the E2E drawing, BOM, part and assembly, and left in Assigned status for production follow-up.

18. Delivery & Service Tracking

This read-only workspace covers Finished Goods Status, Dispatch Tracking, Invoice Tracking and Service & Warranty Tracking. It shows project-wise finished-goods readiness, dispatch references, invoice/payment context, installation, warranty and service references. Filters and search are available; no delivery, invoice or service transaction is created.

19. Reports & Analytics

Reports provides eight analytical views: Project Reports, Design Progress, CAD / Drawing Reports, BOM Reports, Cost Reports, Approval Reports, Change Reports and Workload / Delay Reports. Each view supports date range, project, engineer/domain and status filters where applicable, KPI cards, charts, tables and CSV export.

Charts include bars, donuts, funnels, heatmaps, progress bars, stacked bars, treemaps and trend lines. Report data is seeded analytical data; newly created local records do not automatically rewrite report aggregates. Export is client-side CSV.

20. Settings

Settings is a configuration workspace with 19 independent categories: Overview, Approval Matrix, Approval Rules, Budget Control, 3-Way Matching, Tax Settings, Currency & Exchange Rates, Payment Terms, HSN / SAC, Items / Products, Logistics Defaults, PDF / Document Defaults, Terms & Conditions, Company / Organization, Document Numbering, Workflow Settings, Notifications, Audit / Security and Access Management.

Each category has its own sections, fields and editable lists such as approval levels, rules, tax rates, currencies, payment terms, HSN/SAC codes and item settings. Changes persist locally and are summarized on the overview. These settings are frontend reference values; they do not alter backend authorisation or accounting behaviour.

21. Common interaction standards

Lists and registers

  • Search covers the primary identifiers and names shown in the table.
  • Filters combine with search and reset explicitly.
  • Sortable headers toggle ascending and descending order.
  • Pagination offers row-count options and page navigation.
  • Three-dot action menus open on the row and close on outside click or Escape.
  • Wide tables scroll horizontally inside the table card.

Forms and decisions

  • Required fields are marked and validated before save.
  • Errors appear inline and in a form-level alert.
  • Destructive actions ask for confirmation where data would be lost.
  • Comments are mandatory for request-changes and reject decisions.
  • Successful actions raise a toast and update the register immediately.
  • Browser refresh preserves the current state.

22. Known boundaries

BoundaryWhat it means in use
No backend or databaseAll records live in the browser. There is no multi-user concurrency, server authorization or audit-grade event log.
No ERP transactionsTransfers and handovers create local mock references only. No purchase requisition, purchase order, production order, invoice or dispatch is created.
No engineering computationCalculations and analysis are recorded evidence. The application does not compute stress, thermal, modal, fatigue or tolerance results and does not certify simulation accuracy.
Analytics are seededDashboard and report aggregates are demonstration data and do not recompute from every local record.
Tracking is read-onlyManufacturing and delivery tracking datasets are not automatically fed by new projects, releases or transfers.
CAD preview is format-limitedSVG and raster files preview in the browser; native CAD geometry is not rendered.
