Those records were verified by persisted fields and linked workflow state, not by a complete per-field change log.

9. Limitations and follow-ups

  • Frontend-only simulation. All state is browser localStorage. There is no backend, authentication, real payment, carrier booking, e-way bill, warranty registration or ERP transaction.
  • Revision propagation. ECR-2026-010 is released with a proposed Rev B seal upgrade and the production handover explicitly instructs Rev B incorporation. The BOM and drawing master registers still show Rev A; automatic master-revision propagation is a follow-up.
  • Master status propagation. DES-2026-018 and the drawing/review are Released, but the linked part and assembly master records retain “In Design”; there is no automatic design-to-master status update in this frontend.
  • Cost estimate status. CST-2026-008 remains In Progress even though its INR 19,96,000 total is available; there is no separate approval gate applied to it in this run.
  • Text issue retained. Concept C’s name contains “hydrid” instead of “hybrid”; it was left unchanged to preserve the tested record.
  • Audit coverage. Event-level audit is present for workflow records; several master registers expose only created/updated metadata, as noted in section 5.
  • Invoice correction method. The invoice UI has no edit action for net value; the audited commercial correction was applied to persisted invoice state and then verified through the normal invoice UI. This is the one record correction not made through an ordinary form submission.
